## English

PhD student Xuyang Wu presents the invited paper “Distributed Fenchel Dual Gradient Methods Enabling a Smoothing Technique for Nonsmooth Optimization” at the 57th IEEE Conference on Decision and Control in Miami, in a session chaired by Jie Lu.

博士生吴旭阳在美国迈阿密举行的第 57 届 IEEE 决策与控制会议（CDC）上宣讲邀请论文《Distributed Fenchel Dual Gradient Methods Enabling a Smoothing Technique for Nonsmooth Optimization》，陆疌担任该分会场主席。
