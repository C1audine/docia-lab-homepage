## English

PhD student Zichong Ou gives an oral presentation of “A Zeroth-Order Proximal Algorithm for Consensus Optimization”, joint work with labmate Chengan Wang, at the 63rd IEEE Conference on Decision and Control in Milan.

## 中文

博士生欧子翀在意大利米兰举行的第 63 届 IEEE 决策与控制会议（CDC）上，口头报告了与同课题组王成安合作完成的论文《A Zeroth-Order Proximal Algorithm for Consensus Optimization》。
