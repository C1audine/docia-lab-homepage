## English

Consensus algorithms connect the behavior of an entire network to its communication graph. The playground makes this connection tangible: a node transfers load only to its neighbors, yet repeated exchanges can balance all eight nodes.

## 中文

一致性算法把整个网络的行为与它的通信图联系起来。首页的实验让这一联系变得直观：节点只与邻居交换负载，但重复交换就能让八个节点全部均衡。
