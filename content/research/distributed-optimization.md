## English

In a distributed problem, each agent holds part of the data or objective. Our work studies algorithms that coordinate these local updates, including methods for nonsmooth objectives, coupling constraints, and changing communication networks.

## 中文

在分布式问题中，每个智能体只掌握一部分数据或目标。我们研究协调这些局部更新的算法，涵盖非光滑目标、耦合约束以及动态变化的通信网络。
